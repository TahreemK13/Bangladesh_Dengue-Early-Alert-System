What a warning means

Activation threshold

Each division's action threshold is 1.5 × the median admissions for the same month in earlier years, as a weekly rate, and at least 20 a week. Crossing it means case counts are unusually high for the time of year but not that hospitals are overwhelmed.

What the warning is for

In Sri Lanka's protocol, funding is released when cases actually cross the threshold. This tool warns when crossing is likely within the next weeks (a median of 3 weeks earlier in the backtest), so early actions can start before the peak: stocking IV saline at the hospitals above, clearing breeding sites and getting the word out. Bangladesh has no such protocol yet; this shows how one could work.

Why weather is not an input

Rain mostly marks the season, and the threshold and calendar already carry that. Admissions in recent weeks carry the rest. In the backtest, adding rainfall, temperature and humidity did not improve forecasts 2–6 weeks ahead. The Now view shows a weather-only number as context only.

How it would update

This prototype is rebuilt by hand. In operation, each DGHS daily report would be saved (they disappear from the site), the model rerun every Monday after the week closes, and this page republished. Subscribers would get an email only when a division's status changes, and a division would step down only after 2 calm weeks. If reports stop arriving, divisions show unknown: feed stopped and the maintainers are alerted. No DGHS reports are reachable after 3 September 2026, which is why the Now view is unknown everywhere.

Hospital list

554 public hospitals with beds from DGHS's hospital list. Dhaka Medical College (2,600 beds), Shaheed Suhrawardy (850) and Kuwait-Bangladesh Friendship (200) were missing and were added by hand after checking DGHS's 2019 facility lists. A duplicate Mugda entry was removed, and four medical college hospitals were moved to their exact OpenStreetMap locations. Beds are official counts, not beds in use.

Data: DGHS daily admissions by division via Kaggle (2022–Oct 2025); Mendeley monthly by division (2020–2021); OpenDengue V1.3 national series; Open-Meteo ERA5 weather at division headquarters; WorldPop 2020 1 km population; Malaria Atlas Project 2020 motorized friction surface; OpenStreetMap health facilities via HDX (ODbL); HDX COD-AB boundaries. Threshold rule adapted from the Sri Lanka Red Cross Society's simplified Early Action Protocol for dengue (IFRC, 2025). Site choice follows the location-allocation design of Lippi et al., Int J Health Geogr 2020. Public hospitals and beds: DGHS Hospital Services list (hospitaldghs.gov.bd), with additions from DGHS facility lists, hospital sites and press reports. Saline: 2.5 L per admitted patient a day (Health Services Division planning figure, reported by TBS, 2024) and a 4.9-day average stay (PLOS Neglected Tropical Diseases, 2022).
